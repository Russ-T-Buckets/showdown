📅 Schedule
Each team plays 12 games vs each division rival (four 3-game series) and 3 games vs a selection of non-division opponents — 48 division + 33 cross-division = 81 total.
⚾ Pitching
Each series uses a pre-set pitching script locked to G1, G2, or G3. Starters pitch their scripted innings — no mid-game decisions. Assign W/L/SV at the end of each game.
🏃 Base Running
Speed ratings: A (fast) · B (average) · C (slow). Speed A runners score from 2nd on a single. Stolen bases: 2nd base only — click SB 2B and choose safe or caught.
🧤 Fielding
Each player has a fielding rating. Catchers range 2–11, infielders 0–5, outfielders 0–3. The defensive team's C / IF / OF totals show in the scorebug during play. Higher = better range and arm.
🔢 Key Rules
· HR: batter and all runners score. Bases clear.
· 3B: all runners score. Batter to 3rd.
· 2B: runners on 2nd/3rd score. Runner on 1st to 3rd. Batter to 2nd.
· 1B: runner on 3rd scores. Others advance one base. Speed A runner on 2nd also scores.
· BB: only forced runners advance (chain from 1st). Unforced runners hold.
· GB <2 outs: all runners advance one base, batter out at 1st.
· GB 2 outs: runners hold, batter out.
· FB: runners hold. If runner on 3rd and <2 outs, SAC fly popup appears.
· SAC: requires runner on 3rd, <2 outs. Batter always out. If runner thrown out at home, 2 outs recorded.
· DP: requires runner on 1st, <2 outs. 2 outs recorded — batter + one runner.
· SB: 2nd base only, no stealing 3rd or home.
· Speed A on 1st: must attempt steal of 2nd when open.
· Speed A on 2nd: scores on a single automatically.
· Walkoff: home team takes lead in bottom 9th or later — game ends immediately.
